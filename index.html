Star of the Moment

Give a date, a time and a place. This widget computes the real star that was closest to directly overhead at that moment — the same astronomy that powers astarnamed.com. Built for memorial pages, for people and pets; free to use anywhere.

Embed it on your site

Hosted version, always up to date:

<iframe src="https://astarnamed.com/embed/death-sky"
  style="width:100%;max-width:560px;height:640px;border:0"
  title="Star of the moment"></iframe>

Or self-host the single file — star-widget.html in this repo has no dependencies, no cookies, no tracking.

The star is real; the name is a memorial — commercial naming is not recognized by the International Astronomical Union.
